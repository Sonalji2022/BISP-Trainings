Most important beginner rule:
If the parent controls a subsidiary, consolidation does not simply take 80% or 70% of every line. For this simplified controlled-subsidiary example, the group brings in 100% of the subsidiary's assets, liabilities, income and expenses, then separately presents the portion belonging to outside shareholders as NCI.

What is NCI?
NCI means Non-controlling Interest. Older material may call it minority interest. It represents the part of a controlled subsidiary that belongs to shareholders outside the parent group.
