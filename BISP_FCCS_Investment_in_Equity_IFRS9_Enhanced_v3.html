IFRS Classification and Measurement

Adjust ownership, control, and significant influence to see whether the investment is treated under IFRS 10, IAS 28, or IFRS 9.

IFRS 10

Subsidiary

Control exists. Use full consolidation with line-by-line assets, liabilities, income, expenses, and non-controlling interest where applicable.

IAS 28

Associate / Joint Venture

Significant influence without control. Use the equity method and present a single investment line in the Balance Sheet.

IFRS 9

Passive Equity Investment

No control or significant influence. Measure at fair value through profit or loss, or eligible FVOCI when elected.

Current conclusion: IAS 28 equity method.

Balance Sheet impact

The associate's assets and liabilities are not added line by line. The consolidated Balance Sheet shows one line: Investment in Equity Companies.

Closing investment = Opening investment + Share of profit − Dividends − Impairment.

Income Statement impact

The investor recognises its share of the associate's profit as equity pickup income. In this case, 40% of $2,000 equals $800.

Dividends reduce the carrying value; they are not recognised again as investment income under the equity method.

IFRS 9 Context — and Why IAS 28 Matters Here
Important distinction: A 40% holding with significant influence is normally accounted for using the equity method under IAS 28, not as a standard IFRS 9 equity investment. IFRS 9 mainly governs equity investments that are outside the scope of consolidation and equity-method standards.

IAS 28 — Associate / Joint Venture

Use when the investor has significant influence but not control. The investment starts at cost and is adjusted for the investor's share of post-acquisition profit or loss, dividends, OCI, and impairment.

This workbook's 40% equity-method case fits this model.

IFRS 9 — Passive Equity Investment

Use when the investment is not a subsidiary, associate, or joint venture. Equity instruments are generally measured at fair value through profit or loss, with an irrevocable FVOCI election available for qualifying non-trading equity investments.
