Visual: what changes on acquisition?

New learner rule

A parent does not simply add its “investment in subsidiary” to the target’s assets. In consolidation, the investment is eliminated against the acquired equity/net assets. The target’s underlying assets and liabilities enter the group balance sheet, and any residual may become goodwill.

Parent investment account → eliminated\nTarget identifiable net assets → consolidated\nResidual purchase premium → goodwill\nOutside ownership → NCI

Beginner explanation

Imagine buying a business for ₹23,000 cr. If the fair value of identifiable assets less liabilities is ₹19,000 cr, the unexplained premium is ₹4,000 cr. Under acquisition accounting, that premium is generally recognized as goodwill, subject to the relevant IFRS 3 measurement rules.

Pre-acquisition vs post-acquisition profit

Why split the year?

If control is acquired part-way through a reporting year, only results from the acquisition date onward are consolidated as subsidiary post-acquisition results. The pre-acquisition period helps explain opening retained earnings / net assets and purchase accounting.

Before and after acquisition — Balance Sheet, P&L and Cash Flow

Illustrative educational model. It is not a reproduction of Tata/JLR or Tata Steel/Corus published statements.

Interpretation: Acquisition accounting changes the group balance sheet immediately because the target’s assets/liabilities enter consolidation. The P&L changes from the acquisition date onward. In the cash flow statement, the purchase consideration—net of cash acquired—is generally shown within investing activities, while the acquired business’s post-acquisition operating cash flows enter the group cash flow.

Historical sources & learning notes

Tata Motors / Jaguar Land Rover

Tata Motors states that the Jaguar Land Rover acquisition was consummated on 2 June 2008 for a purchase consideration of US$2.3 billion on a cash-free, debt-free basis. Tata Motors also reported FY2008-09 consolidated gross revenue of ₹74,151.21 crore versus ₹40,340.79 crore in FY2007-08 and explicitly cautioned that the periods were not comparable because of the JLR acquisition.

Tata Motors acquisition clarification · Tata Motors FY2008-09 results

Tata Steel / Corus

Tata Steel announced completion of its Corus acquisition on 2 April 2007 for £6.2 billion (US$12 billion) at 608 pence per ordinary share. Tata Steel’s 2007-08 annual report states that Corus financial statements were included in consolidated results from 2 April 2007, making the previous year not directly comparable.
