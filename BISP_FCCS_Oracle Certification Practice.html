About BISP Training

BISP Trainings provides case-study-oriented learning for Oracle EPM solutions, including Financial Consolidation and Close, Planning, Profitability and Cost Management, Enterprise Data Management, Essbase and related integration technologies.

The learning approach combines functional consolidation concepts, Oracle application configuration, hands-on exercises, troubleshooting scenarios, interview preparation and implementation-focused practice.
