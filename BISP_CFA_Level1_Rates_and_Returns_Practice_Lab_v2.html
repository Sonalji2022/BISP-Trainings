Historically Relevant Exam-Style Themes

ThemeOriginal CFA-style prompt (not a past question)What it tests
Holding-period returnAn investor buys a share, receives a dividend, and sells it later. Which return best measures the investment result?Total return = price change + income.
Geometric vs arithmeticA portfolio experiences large positive and negative annual returns. Which average best describes compound wealth growth?Geometric mean and volatility drag.
Money-weighted vs time-weightedA client makes a large contribution immediately before a market decline. Which measure is more appropriate for evaluating the manager?Cash-flow timing and manager evaluation.
AnnualizationA security earns a stated return over a period shorter than one year. What is the annualized compounded return?Exponent based on periods per year.
Real returnGiven nominal portfolio performance and inflation, calculate growth in purchasing power.Exact Fisher relation.
Continuous compoundingGiven beginning and ending prices, calculate the continuously compounded return.Natural logarithm transformation.
Effective vs stated rateA bank quotes a nominal rate compounded multiple times per year. Which effective annual rate is closest?Compounding frequency.

Concept linkage, not a compliance checklist: CFA “Rates and Returns” is an investment-measurement topic. The accounting standards below use related ideas such as effective interest, discounting, present value and fair value. Their accounting requirements are broader and should be studied from the applicable standard.

IFRS / IAS / US GAAP Crosswalk

CFA conceptIFRS / IAS linkageUS GAAP linkagePractical connection
Effective interest / compounded yieldIFRS 9 — effective interest method for relevant amortised-cost measurements and interest recognition.ASC 835 / instrument-specific guidance — interest method concepts are used in debt accounting.Yield calculations explain how carrying amount and interest income/expense evolve.
Discount rate / required rate of returnIAS 36 — discounting in value-in-use calculations.ASC 360 and other valuation guidance — discounted cash-flow concepts may arise depending on measurement context.Required return becomes a valuation discount rate, subject to standard-specific rules.
Fair value and market return inputsIFRS 13 — fair value measurement framework.ASC 820 — fair value measurement framework.Market yields/risk premiums can be inputs to valuation techniques.
Lease discountingIFRS 16 — lease liabilities use a specified discount rate framework.ASC 842 — lease accounting also uses discount rates.Present-value mechanics rely on rate selection and compounding.
Employee-benefit discountingIAS 19 — discount rate requirements for defined benefit obligations.ASC 715 — discounting is central to defined benefit measurements.Long-dated obligations are highly sensitive to discount rates.
Credit-adjusted returns / yieldIFRS 9 — expected credit-loss and credit-impaired asset concepts interact with interest recognition.ASC 326 — expected credit loss accounting for financial assets.Credit risk affects expected cash flows and investor-required return, though accounting mechanics differ.
