ARCHITECT / CRO CAPSTONE

GlobalBank Group — Enterprise Risk Crisis

You are the CRO team during a synchronized recession, market shock, deposit runoff, cyber incident, model drift and correlation breakdown. All outputs depend on your assumptions.

Capstone Problem Statement — “72 Hours to the Board Risk Committee”

GlobalBank Group is a diversified financial institution with approximately $120B assets, $72B credit exposure, $8B trading assets, $82B deposits, $11B HQLA and $9B available capital. The Board approved a moderate risk appetite, but six risk events are now developing at the same time.

EventWhat has changedYour CRO question
Credit deteriorationCorporate defaults are rising and a large share of exposure is concentrated in cyclical sectors.How much does stressed PD/LGD consume capital, and does concentration create a non-linear loss?
Market dislocationTrading assets fall sharply, volatility jumps and hedge effectiveness deteriorates.What is the net market/counterparty loss after hedge and volatility assumptions?
Funding pressureRetail and wholesale deposits run off while margin calls consume liquidity.Does HQLA plus contingent funding cover stressed outflows?
Cyber outageA ransomware event interrupts payment processing and creates direct and business-interruption losses.How much operational loss must be added to the financial-risk stress?
Model uncertaintyRisk models show drift and management overrides are increasing.What explicit model-risk add-on should be carried?
Correlation breakdownCredit and market losses become more correlated as liquidity disappears.How much diversification survives in severe stress?

Your mandate: create a defensible enterprise stress result, identify threshold breaches, decide whether recovery triggers are reached, prioritize management actions, and prepare a Board Risk Committee recommendation. The exercise is intentionally interactive: changing any assumption changes the risk decision.

Required deliverables: (1) quantified standalone risks, (2) stressed aggregate loss, (3) liquidity coverage proxy, (4) capital-consumption ratio, (5) appetite/recovery breaches, (6) prioritized CRO actions, (7) challenge of correlation/model assumptions, and (8) Board escalation narrative.
