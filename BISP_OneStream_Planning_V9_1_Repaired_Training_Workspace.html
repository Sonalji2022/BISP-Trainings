Simulator design basis

Workflow Profiles drive data loading, transformation assignment, review and certification; Cubes control storage/calculation/translation/consolidation; Cube Views use workflow/POV-driven presentation and calculations; Finance Rules handle multidimensional calculations; Parser/Derivative rules belong to source-data processing; Extensibility rules support generalized/Data Management automation. The simulator selects the corresponding behavior instead of forcing every lab into a financial calculation.
