FCCS Solution Blueprint from the RTM

Application

12 periods, January fiscal start, 2018–2030 target range in source, HYTD enabled, application name FCCsApp.

Metadata

Entity, Account, Movement and Custom dimensions with source-to-seeded mappings.

Currency

USD primary reporting currency with 31 workbook currencies marked in use; CTA and secondary currency require final decisions.

Consolidation

Translation and intercompany elimination are referenced; manual journals are referenced for equity elimination and selected adjustments.

Ownership

Most entities assumed 100%; a 50% ownership example exists. Ownership Management remains traceable as a design decision.

Data Integration

Actuals plus Plan/Forecast requirement with mapping and potential non-GL source needs.

Security

Viewer, User, Power User and Service Administrator patterns with entity/data-source based access.

Reporting

Balance Sheet, P&L, BS cross-cast and Cash Flow Custom3 cross-cast views are listed.

Source Workbook Coverage

Questionnaire

Functional application questions covering application creation, FCCS configuration, currency, journals, intercompany, ownership, integration, workflow and security.

Entities

Entity hierarchy includes FCCS_Total Geography and management levels Line of Business → Sub Line of Business → Division → Sub Division → Group → Cost Center.

Ownership report

Entities not mentioned are noted as 100% owned; a 50% ownership example is provided for Brauhasse across periods.

Accounts

Seeded FCCS account examples include FCCS_Balance Sheet, FCCS_Total Assets, FCCS_CTA and FCCS_EPUSource with metadata properties.

Movements

Seeded Movement examples include FCCS_No Movement and FCCS_ClosingBalance.

Currencies

31 currencies are marked in use: AOA, ARS, AUD, BRL, BWP, CHF, DZD, ETB, EUR, GBP, GHC, INR, KES, LSL, MAD, MUR, MWK, MXN, MZM, NAD, NGN, RON, RUB, SDP, SZL, TZS, UGX, USD, VND, ZAR, ZMK.

Reports and forms

10BS – Balance sheet; 10PL – Profit and loss; BS_CC – BS cross cast; CF_C3_CC – Cash Flow Custom 3 Cross Cast

Users & Groups

Role patterns include Viewer, User, Power User and Service Administrator. Questionnaire references roughly 200 users.

Calculated accounts / Ratios

Templates exist but are marked “To be completed”; corresponding RTM rows are Open.

Custom3 (GAAP)

Custom3 examples include USGAAPAdj, CJE, HubAdj and Input and are linked to the multi-GAAP architecture decision.
