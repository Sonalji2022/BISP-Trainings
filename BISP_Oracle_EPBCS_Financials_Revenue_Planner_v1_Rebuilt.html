Revenue-to-Cash Logic

Revenue drives gross profit. Credit sales create accounts receivable based on DSO. COGS drives inventory and accounts payable assumptions. Operating profit and working-capital movements influence operating cash flow, while closing cash updates the Balance Sheet.

Expected Smart View Output

Business Rule Sequence

  1. Prepare Plan / initialize FY25 plan periods
  2. Read selected actual trend basis
  3. Apply increase or decrease assumptions
  4. Calculate Product Revenue = Volume × ASP
  5. Calculate adjustments, COGS and Gross Profit
  6. Calculate working capital and cash impact
  7. Aggregate and validate
