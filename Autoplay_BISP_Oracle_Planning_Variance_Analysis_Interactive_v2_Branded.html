Scenario 1 — Basic Actual vs Budget

Formula

Absolute Variance = Actual - Budget Variance % = (Actual - Budget) / Budget × 100 Favourability depends on account nature: Revenue: Higher Actual is favourable Expense: Lower Actual is favourable

Scenario 2 — Flexible Budget

Why Flexible Budget?

A static budget is based on planned activity. A flexible budget recalculates the allowed cost at actual activity, separating volume impact from spending or efficiency impact.

Static Budget = Budget Units × Budget Rate + Fixed Cost Flexible Budget = Actual Units × Budget Rate + Fixed Cost Activity Variance = Flexible Budget - Static Budget Spending Variance = Actual Cost - Flexible Budget

Actual vs Budget

Absolute Variance = Actual - Budget Variance % = (Actual - Budget) / Budget × 100

Example: Actual Revenue 1,200; Budget Revenue 1,000.

Absolute Variance = 200; Variance % = 20%. For a revenue account, this is favourable.

Flexible Budget

Static Budget = Budget Volume × Budget Rate + Fixed Cost Flexible Budget = Actual Volume × Budget Rate + Fixed Cost Activity Variance = Flexible Budget - Static Budget Spending Variance = Actual Cost - Flexible Budget
Planning design: Store Budget Volume, Actual Volume, Standard Rate, Fixed Cost and Actual Cost as separate measures. Calculate activity and spending effects into dedicated Variance Driver members.

Price–Volume–Mix

Price Variance = Actual Quantity × (Actual Price - Budget Price) Volume Variance = (Actual Total Quantity - Budget Total Quantity) × Budget Weighted Margin Mix Variance = Actual Total Quantity × (Actual Mix - Budget Mix) × Budget Unit Margin
Planning design: Product, Customer, Channel and Geography dimensions should be stored at sufficient detail to explain mix movement without creating unnecessary cube sparsity.

FX / Constant Currency

Actual at Actual FX = Local Actual / Actual FX Rate Actual at Budget FX = Local Actual / Budget FX Rate Operational Variance = Actual at Budget FX - Budget FX Variance = Actual at Actual FX - Actual at Budget FX
Planning design: Separate local currency, reporting currency and constant-currency measures. Keep rate type and translation method aligned with financial reporting policy.

Workforce Bridge

Headcount Impact = (Actual HC - Budget HC) × Budget Cost per Employee Rate Impact = Actual HC × (Actual Rate - Budget Rate) Bonus Impact = Actual HC × Actual Salary × (Actual Bonus % - Budget Bonus %)
Planning design: Integrate Workforce Planning data by Employee, Job or Grade, then aggregate driver impacts for FP&A reporting.

CapEx Timing & Depreciation

Spend Variance = Actual CapEx - Budget CapEx Annual Depreciation = Capitalized Cost / Useful Life Period Depreciation = Annual Depreciation × Months in Service / 12 Timing Variance = Actual Depreciation - Budget Depreciation after isolating spend impact
Planning design: Capture project, asset class, in-service month, useful life, capitalization status and depreciation method.
