  10. How do Management Reporting and Smart View support profitability analysis?

Scenario 1: Loss-Making Customer

A high-revenue customer appears unprofitable after logistics and support costs are allocated.

Interview approach: validate drivers, trace allocations, confirm cost causality and recommend commercial actions.

Scenario 2: Allocation Imbalance

Rule execution completes, but destination totals do not equal source totals.

Interview approach: inspect rule scope, exclusions, offset logic, driver data and rounding.
