Metadata Loading using REST API

Integrate external governance platforms, portals or orchestration tools with Oracle Planning jobs.

1. AuthenticateObtain valid credentials or an OAuth token for the Oracle EPM environment.

Integrated Financial Statements & Currency Conversion

Profit & Loss Business Rules

Average-rate translation
1

Revenue

Sales Volume × Average Selling Price

2

COGS

Material + Labour + Manufacturing Overhead

3

Operating Expenses

Employee, selling, marketing, admin and R&D

4

EBITDA / EBIT

Gross Profit − Operating Expense − D&A

5

Tax / Net Income

PBT − Current and Deferred Tax

Key Formulas

Gross RevenueSales Volume × Average Selling Price
Net RevenueGross Revenue − Discounts − Returns
Gross ProfitNet Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold
EBITDAGross Profit − Operating Expenses
Net IncomeProfit Before Tax − Income Tax Expense

Essbase Rule

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYear,@RELATIVE("Base Entities",0)) "Gross Revenue" = "Sales Volume" * "Average Selling Price"; "Net Revenue" = "Gross Revenue" - "Sales Discounts" - "Sales Returns"; "Gross Profit" = "Net Revenue" - "Cost of Goods Sold"; "EBITDA" = "Gross Profit" - "Operating Expenses"; "Net Income" = "Profit Before Tax" - "Income Tax Expense"; ENDFIX

Balance Sheet Business Rules

Working-capital and roll-forward driven
1

Receivables

Credit Sales × DSO ÷ Days in Period

2

Inventory

Raw Material + WIP + Finished Goods

3

Payables

Credit Purchases × DPO ÷ Days in Period

4

PP&E and Debt

Opening + Additions − Disposals / Repayments

5

Retained Earnings

Opening + Net Income − Dividends

Key Formulas

Trade ReceivablesCredit Sales × DSO ÷ Days in Period
Total InventoryRaw Material + WIP + Finished Goods
Net Fixed AssetsClosing Gross Fixed Assets − Accumulated Depreciation
Closing Retained EarningsOpening RE + Net Income − Dividends
BS CheckTotal Assets − Total Liabilities − Total Equity

Essbase Rule

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYear,@RELATIVE("Base Entities",0)) "Trade Receivables" = "Credit Sales" * "Days Sales Outstanding" / "Days in Period"; "Inventory" = "Cost of Goods Sold" * "Inventory Days" / "Days in Period"; "Trade Payables" = "Credit Purchases" * "Days Payable Outstanding" / "Days in Period"; "Net Fixed Assets" = "Opening Net Fixed Assets" + "Capital Additions" - "Asset Disposals" - "Depreciation Expense"; "Closing Retained Earnings" = "Opening Retained Earnings" + "Net Income" - "Dividends Declared"; ENDFIX

Cash Flow Business Rules

Indirect method
1

CFO

Net Income + Noncash Items ± Working Capital

2

CFI

CapEx, disposals, investments and acquisitions

3

CFF

Borrowings, repayments, share issue and dividends

4

Closing Cash

Opening Cash + CFO + CFI + CFF + FX Effect

5

Reconciliation

Balance Sheet Cash = Cash Flow Closing Cash

Key Formulas

Change in Receivables−(Closing AR − Opening AR)
Change in Inventory−(Closing Inventory − Opening Inventory)
Change in PayablesClosing AP − Opening AP
Net Cash MovementCFO + CFI + CFF
Closing CashOpening Cash + Net Movement + FX Effect

Essbase Rule

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYear,@RELATIVE("Base Entities",0)) "CF Change in Receivables" = -1 * ("Closing Trade Receivables" - "Opening Trade Receivables"); "CF Change in Inventory" = -1 * ("Closing Inventory" - "Opening Inventory"); "CF Change in Payables" = "Closing Trade Payables" - "Opening Trade Payables"; "Net Cash from Operating Activities" = "Net Income" + "Depreciation Expense" + "CF Change in Receivables" + "CF Change in Inventory" + "CF Change in Payables"; "Closing Cash Before FX" = "Opening Cash" + "Net Increase Decrease in Cash"; ENDFIX

Configure Attribute Dimensions

  1. Create the Product and Customer base dimensions.
  2. Create attribute dimensions such as Fuel Type, Brand, Region and Customer Tier.
  3. Define attribute members and aliases.
  4. Associate each attribute dimension with its base dimension.
  5. Assign attribute values to level-zero Product or Customer members.
  6. Refresh the database and validate attribute associations.
  7. Use attributes in forms, ad hoc analysis, reports and dashboards.
Design rule: Attribute dimensions are descriptive and should not be used as independent planning input dimensions.

Calculation Script

BR_Forecast_Seeding

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYr,@RELATIVE("All Entities",0)) FIX("Jan":"&ActualThrough") DATACOPY "Actual" TO "Forecast"; ENDFIX FIX("&FirstForecastMonth":"Dec") "Revenue" = "Vehicle_Volume" * "ASP" - "Dealer_Incentive"; "Material_Cost" = "Production_Units" * "Standard_Material_Cost"; ENDFIX ENDFIX

BR_DataCopy_Forecast

FIX(&Year,&PeriodRange,@RELATIVE(&Entity,0)) CLEARDATA "Submitted"; DATACOPY "Working" TO "Submitted"; ENDFIX /* Validate source/target POV and prevent copying into approved periods. */

BR_Calc_IncomeStatement

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYr,@RELATIVE("All Entities",0)) "Revenue" = "Sales_Volume" * "ASP" - "Dealer_Incentive"; "COGS" = "Material_Cost" + "Labor_Cost" + "Manufacturing_Overhead"; "Gross_Margin" = "Revenue" - "COGS"; "EBITDA" = "Gross_Margin" - "Operating_Expense"; "EBIT" = "EBITDA" - "Depreciation"; "Net_Income" = "Profit_Before_Tax" - "Tax_Expense"; ENDFIX

BR_Calc_BalanceSheet

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYr,@RELATIVE("All Entities",0)) "Accounts_Receivable" = "Opening_AR" + "Credit_Sales" - "Collections"; "Inventory" = "Opening_Inventory" + "Production_Cost" - "COGS"; "Accounts_Payable" = "Opening_AP" + "Purchases" - "Supplier_Payments"; "Net_PP&E" = "Opening_PP&E" + "CapEx" - "Depreciation" - "Asset_Disposals"; "Retained_Earnings" = "Opening_RE" + "Net_Income" - "Dividends"; ENDFIX

BR_Calc_CashFlow

FIX("Forecast","Working",&CurYr,@RELATIVE("All Entities",0)) "CFO" = "Net_Income" + "Depreciation" - "Change_in_AR" - "Change_in_Inventory" + "Change_in_AP"; "CFI" = - "CapEx" + "Asset_Sale_Proceeds"; "CFF" = "Debt_Drawdown" - "Debt_Repayment" + "Equity_Issued" - "Dividends"; "Closing_Cash" = "Opening_Cash" + "CFO" + "CFI" + "CFF"; ENDFIX

Configure Monthly Application Maintenance

  1. Create user variables for Current Year, Current Period and Forecast Start Month.
  2. Configure metadata refresh jobs for Account, Entity, Product, Customer and other custom dimensions.
  3. Create Data Integration jobs for Oracle Fusion Trial Balance, QuickBooks actuals and FX rates.
  4. Create forecast seeding and Data Copy business rules.
  5. Create a ruleset for Revenue, COGS, Workforce, P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow.
  6. Configure Smart Push or Data Maps for the reporting cube.
  7. Create reconciliation reports and exception thresholds.
  8. Build a Pipeline to sequence jobs, stop on failure and send notifications.
  9. Schedule the Pipeline after source-system close and test restartability.
