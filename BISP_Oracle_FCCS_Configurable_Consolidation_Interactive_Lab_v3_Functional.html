Core FCCS concept: a configurable consolidation ruleset represents a journal, while each rule represents one journal posting line. Rules can redirect targets conditionally by dimensions such as Account, Movement, Intercompany and Data Source.

Example 1 — Subsidiary consolidation and NCI

Parent controls Subsidiary A. Under IFRS 10, consolidate 100% of assets, liabilities, income and expenses, then separately attribute equity and profit to NCI.

Consolidation % = 100% (control)
NCI % = 100% − Ownership %
NCI in net assets = Net Assets × NCI %
NCI share of profit = Subsidiary Profit × NCI %

Example 2 — Acquisition goodwill

Simplified full-goodwill model under IFRS 3. Enter consideration, fair value of NCI, previously held interest and identifiable net assets.

Goodwill = Consideration + FV of NCI + FV of previous interest − FV of identifiable net assets

Acquisition output

120,000

Recognized as goodwill, subject to impairment testing rather than amortization under IFRS.

FCCS mapping: acquisition entries are commonly posted through journals or configured acquisition rules into dedicated goodwill, NCI and acquisition reserve accounts, with Movement and Data Source preserving auditability.

Example 5 — IAS 21 translation and CTA

Translated net assets = (Assets − Liabilities) ÷ Closing Rate
Historical equity = (Assets − Liabilities) ÷ Historical Rate
CTA = Translated net assets − Historical equity
